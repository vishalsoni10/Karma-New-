Jain Chaturmas Event Management

Karma supports Jain Chaturmas programs with structured administration, hospitality and on-ground coordination for Sanghs, organizations and religious communities.

Chaturmas Administration

  • Registration and participant records
  • Attendance and daily coordination
  • Room allotment and accommodation support
  • Guest welcome and hospitality desks
  • Transportation and local logistics
  • Volunteer and team coordination
  • Bahuman, prabhavna and program-flow support
  • Food and Swamivatsalya coordination

Built for Long-Running Programs

Chaturmas requires consistent administration over an extended period. Karma can help centralize records, schedules, guest movement and operational requirements so organizers can focus on the religious program itself.
