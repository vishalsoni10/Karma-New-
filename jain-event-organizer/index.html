Jain Event Organizer in India

Karma Event Management & Hospitality is a Palitana-based Jain event organizer providing disciplined administration, logistics, hospitality and on-ground execution for Jain religious programs across India.

Complete Jain Event Management

We coordinate the operational details that make a Jain event run smoothly: registration, attendance, guest records, room allotment, transportation, accommodation, hospitality desks, volunteers, food coordination, bahuman, prabhavna and event-day supervision.

Jain Events We Support

  • Jain Sangh and Bus Sangh
  • Chaturmas and Paryushan programs
  • Upadhan Tap and religious tapasya programs
  • Jain Yatra and pilgrimage groups
  • Pratishtha, Mahotsav and Dharma Sabha
  • Samvatsari and community programs

Administration, Not Just Decoration

Karma focuses on the administration behind the event: who is arriving, where guests stay, how groups travel, how attendance is recorded, how hospitality is coordinated, and how bahuman and prabhavna are delivered with dignity and accuracy.

Based in Palitana, Serving India

Palitana is a major Jain pilgrimage destination. From our Gujarat base, Karma can coordinate Jain event requirements for organizations and Sanghs planning programs in Gujarat and other parts of India.
