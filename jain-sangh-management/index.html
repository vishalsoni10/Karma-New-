Jain Sangh & Bus Sangh Management

Karma provides end-to-end coordination for Jain Sangh and Bus Sangh programs, connecting registration, guest records, accommodation, transport, hospitality and event-day operations.

Before the Sangh Travels

  • Participant and family registration
  • Group records and attendance planning
  • Room allotment and accommodation lists
  • Vehicle and route planning
  • Pickup, drop and railway or airport coordination

During the Program

  • Welcome and help desks
  • Guest movement and attendance
  • Volunteer coordination
  • Hospitality and meal coordination
  • On-ground operational supervision

For Jain Organizations

Our administration approach is designed for religious programs where accurate records, respectful hospitality and disciplined coordination matter as much as the visible event setup.
