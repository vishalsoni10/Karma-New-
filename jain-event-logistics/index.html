Jain Event Logistics & Transportation

Karma coordinates the movement behind Jain events, from Bus Sangh planning and route coordination to guest pickups, accommodation movement and on-ground transportation.

Logistics Coordination

  • Bus Sangh and group transportation
  • Vehicle planning and route schedules
  • Pickup and drop coordination
  • Railway station and airport transfers
  • Guest movement between accommodation and venues
  • Driver and vehicle coordination
  • Volunteer and route-desk support
  • Event-day movement supervision

Integrated with Sangh Administration

Transportation is connected with registration, attendance, accommodation and hospitality. Karma coordinates these functions together so Jain groups can move safely and efficiently through the program schedule.
